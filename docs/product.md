# Product plan

Condensed from the "After the Dragons: Product and Build Plan" (16 Oct 2026). The plan's revenue
numbers are illustrative guesses, not forecasts.

## Vision

A mood-first book finder that also shows every place to buy, borrow or listen to a book, in every
format, at the best price. Recommendation apps stop at "add to shelf" and retail sites only show their
own price. Readers do the second step by hand in several tabs.

**Audience:** readers aged roughly 18 to 40 who find books on BookTok, plus budget readers who borrow,
buy used or wait for ebook deals.

**Revenue:** affiliate commission first, then ads, then a paid tier (capped near $5 a month by what
competitors charge).

**Not doing:** pirated copies, copied blurbs or reviews, a social network.

## Release 1 (must have)

1. Mood and trope picker, with pace, length and series filters.
2. "If you loved X" search: 3 to 5 picks, each with a reason we wrote.
3. Book page with an offers table: one row per format and store, cheapest marked, library row always shown.
4. Content warnings.
5. Series reading order.
6. Share cards (one tap to an image for TikTok, Instagram, messaging).
7. Weekly email: three picks and one deal.

## Release 2

Library-first toggle, price-drop alerts, palette-cleanser mode ("the opposite of X"), buy-as-a-set
reading lists, Goodreads/StoryGraph import.

## Skipped on purpose

Social feed and public reviews, reading tracker and stats, chat-style AI as the main interface, and
any download or borrow-from-us feature. AI helps behind the scenes to draft tags that a person checks.

## Quality rules

- Every reason is original text.
- Every pick is checked against at least two sources before it goes live.
- Sponsored placements are labelled and never mixed into mood results.
- Offers sort by lowest total price, ties go to the higher-paying program, and a cheaper option is never hidden.
- Every page carries a plain affiliate notice.

## Launch genres

Romance and romantasy, cozy and epic fantasy, science fiction. Thriller and horror in phase two.

## Roadmap

Four phases with three gates; each gate must be met before the next phase starts. Gate 3 is when to
apply to Journey by Mediavine (accepts sites from about 1,000 sessions a month).

## Status

Live: static site, offers table, `/go` redirect with click log, 13 reviewed books, review workflow,
share cards (PNG per book), weekly email template. Not yet done: price feeds, affiliate approvals,
automated sending, Release 2 features.
